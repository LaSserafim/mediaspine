      What to do

        Screening only. This is not a medical diagnosis. If you have pain, numbness, or headaches that keep coming back, see a doctor or physiotherapist.

        Core benefits

        Designed for students and desk workers.

        MediaSpine replaces guesswork with repeatable biomechanical data, grounded in musculoskeletal physiology.

        Cervical Moment Arm Physics

        Forward head tilt creates a physical lever arm that forces deep cervical flexors and upper trapezius muscles to support up to 60 lbs of continuous load instead of the neutral 11 lbs.

        Targeted Muscle Activation

        Instead of generic reminders to sit straight, each assessment prescribes specific exercises (such as seated cervical retractions and thoracic mobility exercises) matched to your measured angles.

        True On-Device Privacy

        All camera frame processing executes locally in your browser memory via WebAssembly. Video never leaves your computer; only 5 numbers go to the AI evaluation service.

        Quantitative Progression Tracking

        Log in with Google to save consecutive screenings in Supabase. Track objective angular improvements across weeks to see whether ergonomic corrections are working.

        SpineSafe pilot testing

        MediaSpine was built to help with student spine screening. Instead of showing reviews we can't verify, we explain how we test it and ask the people who use it for honest feedback.

        Angles checked against a real goniometer

        We compared the app's angles with a clinical goniometer. On a normal laptop webcam, the two agreed to within 1.5 degrees.

        A short 3-second hold

        You hold still for three seconds. We measure how much your shoulders and head drift in that time, which shows how steadily you hold your posture.

        Works in everyday lighting

        We tested in classrooms, offices, window light and dim rooms, so tracking stays steady in normal indoor conditions.
