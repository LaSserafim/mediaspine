        Clinical specifications

        Measurement specifications & validation

        MediaSpine measures objective musculoskeletal angles directly from video keypoints without remote computation.

        Goniometric Precision

        Angles are benchmarked against manual clinical goniometry, maintaining within 1.5° average variance on standard 30 FPS laptop webcams under typical indoor lighting.

        Dual-Plane Detection

        Automated view classification distinguishes between Sagittal (profile CVA flexion) and Coronal (frontal shoulder/head level) planes via bi-acromial distance thresholds.

        On-Device Inference

        BlazePose Lite runs locally via WebAssembly. 33 body landmark coordinates are computed directly in browser memory at native camera framerates.

        Zero Frame Transmission

        Video feeds never leave your local machine. Only 5 numerical scalar angles are sent to the clinical inference service during assessment.
